AddonSmith Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 21, 2026
Developer: RafTechz
Contact: rafftechz@gmail.com

AddonSmith is an app for creating Minecraft Bedrock add-ons. This policy explains what information the app handles and what it never touches. The short version: no accounts, no sign-up, your creations stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

What stays on your device

Everything you create — projects, blocks, items, mobs, textures, settings, and your "remove ads" purchase status — is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere unless you explicitly use the Showcase (below). Deleting the app deletes this data.

Accounts (optional)

You can use AddonSmith without an account. If you choose to create an account or sign in (with an email and password, or with Google), we store, through our authentication provider (Supabase):

  • Your email address and a display name (any nickname you choose)
  • If you sign in with Google, the basic profile Google returns (name, email)

Your password is never seen or stored by us — it is hashed by Supabase. An account is used only to identify you in the greeting and to attribute add-ons you share to the Showcase. Your creations stay on your device; an account does not upload them.

Deleting your account: email us at the address above and we will delete your account and its data.

The community Showcase (optional)

If you choose to upload an add-on to the Showcase, the following is stored on our servers (hosted by Supabase) and shown publicly to all users of the app:

  • The add-on file and its thumbnail image
  • The title, description, and creator name you type in (you can use any nickname — we never ask for your real name)
  • A random device identifier generated by the app (a random number that is not linked to your identity, your Google account, or your hardware)

When you use the Showcase (uploading, downloading, or reporting), your IP address is processed briefly on the server to prevent abuse (rate limiting and spam protection). It is used only for counting recent requests and is not used to identify or profile you.

Browsing and creating add-ons requires no Showcase interaction at all — the app works fully offline.

Removing an upload: email us at the address above with the add-on's title and we will delete it.

Advertising

AddonSmith shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. This collection is governed by Google's own policies:

  • How Google uses data
  • Google Privacy Policy

The one-time "remove ads" purchase disables all ads permanently.

Purchases

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

Children's privacy

AddonSmith does not require an account and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The creator name on Showcase uploads is free-text chosen by the user — we encourage everyone, especially younger players, to use a made-up nickname. If you believe a child has posted personal information to the Showcase, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

All network traffic (Showcase and ads) uses HTTPS. Showcase write operations are protected server-side; there are no user accounts and therefore no passwords to lose.

Changes to this policy
